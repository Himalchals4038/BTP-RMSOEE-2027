# Master Overview of Indian Government & Corporate Bond Types (2006–2026)

This document provides a comprehensive taxonomy of all types of government and corporate debt instruments issued in India over the last 20 years. ## 1. Central & State Government Securities (G-Secs)

| Bond Type | Primary Database / Portal | Typical Tenor | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dated G-Secs** | RBI DBIE / NDS-OM | 5–40 Years | Fixed semi-annual coupon; benchmark sovereign risk-free instrument. |
| **Treasury Bills (T-Bills)** | RBI DBIE / NDS-OM | 91 / 182 / 364 Days | Zero-coupon money market instruments issued at discount and redeemed at par. |
| **Cash Management Bills (CMBs)** | RBI DBIE | < 91 Days | Ultra short-term bills to smooth out temporary government cash mismatches. |
| **Floating Rate Bonds (FRBs)** | RBI Retail Direct | 7–15 Years | Coupon rate periodically reset against benchmarks like 182-day T-Bill yields. |
| **Inflation Indexed Bonds (IIBs)** | RBI DBIE | 10 Years | Principal/coupon adjusted according to CPI/WPI inflation metrics. |
| **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)** | RBI DBIE / NSE / BSE | 8 Years | Denominated in grams of gold; 2.50% annual coupon + capital appreciation tax-free at maturity. |
| **Sovereign Green Bonds (SGrBs)** | RBI DBIE / NSE Debt | 5–30 Years | Sovereign debt earmarked specifically for green/renewable infrastructure. |
| **State Development Loans (SDLs)** | RBI DBIE / NDS-OM | 3–30 Years | Debt issued by individual Indian states to fund state budgetary requirements. |
| **Special SDLs (UDAY Bonds)** | RBI / State Financial Depts | 10–15 Years | Debt issued under UDYAM/UDAY schemes to take over state Discom liabilities. |

## 2. Corporate & Institutional Bond Categories

| Bond Category | Primary Database / Portal | Structure / Tenor | Key Attributes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Senior Secured NCDs** | SEBI Bond Central / NSE / BSE | 1–10 Years | Asset-backed corporate debt with priority charge on company cash flows. |
| **Senior Unsecured NCDs** | SEBI Bond Central / NSE / BSE | 2–10 Years | Unsecured debentures issued by high-rated conglomerates. |
| **AT1 Perpetual Bonds** | SEBI Bond Central / NSE Debt | Perpetual (5/10 yr call) | Loss-absorbing Basel III capital issued by banks; write-down clause on stress. |
| **Tier-2 Subordinated Debt** | SEBI Bond Central / BSE Debt | 10–15 Years | Subordinated capital issued by banks and NBFCs. |
| **PSU Tax-Free Bonds** | SEBI / NSE / BSE | 10, 15, 20 Years | Issued by NHAI, REC, PFC, IRFC with 100% tax-free interest under Sec 10(15)(h). |
| **Sec 54EC Capital Gain Bonds** | RBI / REC / PFC / NHAI | 5 Years | Exempts investors from long-term capital gains tax on property sales. |
| **Convertible Debentures (CCD/OCD)** | SEBI / MCA Filings | 3–10 Years | Mandatory or optional conversion into equity shares. |
| **InvIT & REIT Debt Securities** | SEBI InvIT/REIT Portal | 3–20 Years | Pass-through yield-generating debt instruments for infra and real estate. |
| **Masala Bonds** | RBI ECB Database / LSE / SGX | 3–10 Years | Rupee-denominated bonds issued in overseas markets. |
| **Commercial Papers (CPs)** | RBI / NSDL / CDSL / NSE | 7–365 Days | Short-term unsecured money market debt issued by corporations and NBFCs. |
| **Municipal Bonds** | SEBI Municipal Registry | 5–10 Years | Local authority debt for urban infrastructure (e.g., Pune, Vadodara Municipal Corps). |

## 3. Official Databases & Search Portals

1. **RBI DBIE (Database on Indian Economy)**: [data.rbi.org.in](https://data.rbi.org.in/DBIE/#/dbie/home) — Comprehensive source for G-Secs, T-Bills, SDLs, and macroeconomic statistics.
2. **RBI Retail Direct / NDS-OM**: Direct bidding and secondary market portal for sovereign debt.
3. **SEBI Bond Central**: Unified corporate debt repository detailing issue sizes, ratings, and listing disclosures.
4. **NSE & BSE Debt Market Portals**: Daily wholesale debt market reports, security-wise trade Data, and downloadable Bhavcopies.
